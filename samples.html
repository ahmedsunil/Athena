IMPORTANT: Component Directory

    
For components to work with x-admin.forms.* syntax, they must be in:

    
resources/views/components/admin/forms/

    
Not in:

    
resources/admin-system/components/forms/

    
Move files from resources/admin-system/components/forms/* to resources/views/components/admin/forms/
