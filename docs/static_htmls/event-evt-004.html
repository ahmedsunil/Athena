SS3 Valedictory Service & Send-Off

The Valedictory Service marks the official farewell to our SS3 graduating class. The morning begins with a chapel service of thanksgiving, followed by speeches from the Principal, staff, student body president, and a guest speaker from among our distinguished alumni.

Families are invited to a reception in the Multipurpose Hall after the ceremony. Each graduating student will receive a personalised yearbook. Photography packages will be available from our official photographer on the day.
