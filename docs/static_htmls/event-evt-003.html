Inter-House Sports Day

Inter-House Sports Day is one of the most anticipated events of the school calendar. Students compete across track and field events, relays, and field sports in a day filled with school spirit, friendly rivalry, and celebration.

The day begins at 8:00am with the march-past parade, followed by the opening ceremony and blessing. Events run throughout the day with the final relay and closing ceremony from 3:30pm to 5:00pm. Parents are encouraged to join us from 9:00am. Refreshments will be available for purchase at the venue.
