About Hulhudhuffaaru School

Shaping Tomorrow's Leaders Since 1987

Who We Are

Hulhudhuffaaru School is a premier co-educational institution committed to academic excellence, character development, and community service. Founded in 1987 on the belief that every child deserves a world-class education, we have grown from a modest campus of 120 students to a thriving learning community of over 1,200. Our graduates go on to lead in medicine, law, engineering, the arts, and public service across Nigeria and the world.

We believe that education is not simply the acquisition of knowledge — it is the formation of the whole person. Our curriculum is rigorous and internationally benchmarked, while our pastoral care ensures every student feels known, valued, and supported. From the classroom to the playing field, from the debate stage to the science laboratory, we challenge our students to discover and develop their unique gifts.

Our Motto

"Knowledge, Character, Service"

Mission

To provide a holistic, values-driven education that equips every student with the knowledge, character, and confidence to lead purposeful lives and contribute meaningfully to society.

Vision

To be a leading institution of learning in West Africa, recognised for academic excellence, character formation, and the development of global citizens who serve their communities with integrity and compassion.

Our Story

School History

The story of Hulhudhuffaaru School is a testament to a community's unwavering commitment to its children. Established during a pivotal moment in the history of Raa Atoll, our school has grown from humble beginnings into a leading institution for higher secondary education. The development and success we see today are the direct result of the grit, vision, and tireless hard work of our founding educators and the pioneer community.

Our Foundations: The Great Migration (1993–1995)

The roots of our school are deeply connected to the relocation of the people from the islands of Ungulu and Undoodhoo. In 1993, families began their migration to Hulhudhuffaaru, seeking better facilities and a brighter future. By 1995, the migration was complete, and the island was officially declared inhabited.

During these early transition years, education never faltered. Before a dedicated school building existed, teaching was conducted in temporary spaces. In 1994, classes were held within the Magistrate Court, later moving to the Island Office (now the Island Council). These early days were marked by simple conditions — legend has it the office didn't even have a door at the time — but the passion for learning was boundless.

The Three Pillars: Honoring Our Pioneers

The success of Hulhudhuffaaru School was carried on the shoulders of dedicated teachers who sacrificed much to ensure no student was left behind. Before the migration was finalized, these educators traveled between islands, exchanging places and traveling from Ungulu or Undoodhoo to Hulhudhuffaaru to keep the classrooms running.

We honor the "Three Pillars" whose tireless work laid our academic foundation:

Mr. Mohamed Rasheed — Mathematics

Mr. Ahmed Niyaz — Mathematics & Dhivehi

Mr. Abdul Latheef Umar — Practical Arts & Physical Education

Following the full relocation in 1995, they were joined by Mr. Saeed (Islam), completing the core team that shaped our school's identity. The standard of excellence we strive for today was set by their hands.

Growth and the Modern Era

On May 17, 1995, Hulhudhuffaaru School was formally inaugurated by the then Minister of Education, Dr. Mohamed Latheef. The original "Main School" consisted of just three buildings and six classrooms.

From those six original classrooms, we have expanded into a comprehensive educational center:

Higher Secondary

Our Vision

To be the premier higher secondary school in the North Central Province, producing graduates who are both proud Maldivians and global citizens.

A Legacy Continued

At Hulhudhuffaaru School, we pride ourselves on a holistic education system rooted in Islamic Maldivian identity. As we look toward the future, we remain forever grateful to the pioneer families and teachers from Ungulu and Undoodhoo. Every modern classroom and academic achievement is a tribute to their struggle. We continue to build upon their hard work, ensuring that Hulhudhuffaaru School remains a beacon of hope and opportunity for generations to come.
