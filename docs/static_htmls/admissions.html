Admissions

Overview

We welcome applications from families who share our commitment to academic excellence and character development. Our admissions process is designed to be straightforward and transparent — we want every family to feel supported from their very first enquiry through to enrolment. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis, subject to availability in the year group applied for.
