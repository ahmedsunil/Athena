Cultural Day & Food Fair 2024

Cultural Day 2024 brought the school community together in a joyful celebration of Nigeria's rich heritage. Each class adopted a different state, decorating their classroom and preparing traditional food, music, and cultural displays for visitors to experience.

Highlights included a traditional attire parade, a live drumming ensemble by the music department, and a food fair featuring dishes from over 20 Nigerian states. The event was attended by nearly 800 students, staff, and family members.
